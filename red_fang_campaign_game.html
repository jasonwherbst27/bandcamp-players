How to Play · Riff & Ruin

1. Album-Timed Chapters: Every track on the chosen Red Fang album is a dungeon chamber on the inked map. Hit Play in the bottom Bandcamp player to start the music and the live album clock automatically—or use Pause, Prev/Next, or click any map node to keep the board synced to the song you're hearing.

2. Battle the Virtual Band: Each song is guarded by Aaron Beam, Bryan Giles, David Sullivan, or John Sherman—and all four unite on the closing track. Spend your 3 Stamina each turn playing Attack, Shield, and Arcane cards, rolling the D20 for bonus damage and Encore Crits (Nat 20).

3. Clear Chambers & Trigger Song Events: Attempt each song's unique D20 Skill Check once per track for healing, shields, or bonus damage. Defeat the room boss before the track finishes to claim bonus healing and score; if time runs out while they stand, the band lands a Song-Change Ambush as your hero marches into the next track.

4. Main Quests, Side Quests & Vaults: Clear full albums (Main Quests), singles & compilations (Side Quests), and Deluxe Editions with sealed bonus rooms (Vaults) to unlock permanent Relics saved right in your browser.
